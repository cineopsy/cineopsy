## [ACT 1: VIBRANIUM KYA HAI — 2:00 to 3:30]

**[VISUAL: Wakanda scenes, Black Panther suit, Captain America shield]**

**VOICEOVER:**

"Pehle samjhte hain —
Vibranium exactly kya karta hai.

MCU mein Vibranium ka ek khaas property hai —

**Kinetic energy absorption.**

Matlab — jo bhi energy iske upar aayi,
woh absorb ho jaati hai.
Aur redistribute ho jaati hai.

Captain America ki shield —
ek full swing mein energy store karti hai
aur wapas release karti hai.

Black Panther ka suit —
bullets, blasts, impacts —
sab absorb karke wapas daagta hai.

**Toh surface pe — Vibranium almost indestructible hai.**

Lekin —
aur yahan story interesting hoti hai —

Vibranium external energy rok sakta hai.
**Internal energy nahi.**"

**[VISUAL: Thanos snap, Hulk snap, Tony snap — compare karo]**

**Teen layers ki damage hoti hai:**

**Layer 1 — Kinetic/Physical:**

**Layer 2 — Cosmic Radiation:**
Infinity Stones se nikalne wali energy —
gamma radiation se kaafi zyada powerful —

**Layer 3 — Soul/Reality Overload:**
Jab aap reality, time, space, mind, soul

human body handle nahi kar sakti.

Ab dekhte hain in teeno pe
Vibranium ka effect:"

**[VISUAL: Simple animated breakdown — 3 layers]**

**VOICEOVER:**

"**Layer 1 — Kinetic damage:**
Vibranium rok sakta hai. ✅

**Layer 2 — Cosmic Radiation:**
Yahan problem aati hai.
Vibranium external radiation rokta hai —
lekin stones gauntlet mein HAIN.
Energy andar se generate hoti hai.
Vibranium ke andar se nikalne ka rasta nahi —
woh Tony ke body se guzarti hai.
Partially. ❌

**Layer 3 — Soul/Reality overload:**
Koi bhi metal is cheez se nahi bachaa sakta.
Yeh purely biological/metaphysical hai. ❌

**Toh score:**
Vibranium — 1 out of 3 layers rokta hai."

## [ACT 3: COMPARISON — HULK vs TONY vs THANOS — 5:15 to 6:45]

**[VISUAL: All three snap moments]**

**VOICEOVER:**

"Yahan ek interesting data point hai.

**Thanos ne snap kiya —**
Uru metal ka gauntlet.
Ek haath damaged.
Woh survived — severely weakened.

**Hulk ne snap kiya —**
Stark tech ka gauntlet.
Poora arm damaged permanently.
Survived — kyunki gamma radiation already
uski body mein absorbed rehti hai.

**Tony ne snap kiya —**
Nano-tech armor.
Poora right side damaged.
Death — kyunki human body
itni radiation handle nahi kar sakti.

**Pattern kya hai?**

Survivability directly related hai
body ki radiation tolerance se —
suit se nahi.

Iska matlab —

**Vibranium suit hota toh
surface damage kam hoti.
Lekin internal radiation damage —
almost same rehti.**"
